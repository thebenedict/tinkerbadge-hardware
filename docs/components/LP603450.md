## Design-relevant facts

- Page 1 drawing pixels show black, yellow and red wires, with the middle wire serving the NTC. Numbered mating-contact polarity is absent. Do not infer physical pin numbers from wire colors.
- Capacity is 1100 mAh minimum, 1130 mAh typical. Operating range is 2.75–4.20 V; charging voltage 4.2 V ±50 mV. Maximum charge current is 550 mA and continuous discharge 1100 mA. Charging is allowed at 0–45°C; discharge at −20 to 60°C.
- The PCM overcurrent trip of 2–4.5 A does not protect a 1 A connector. The listed overdischarge resume voltage of 2.50 V is below its 2.75 V detection voltage and needs vendor clarification.
- The NTC is 10 kΩ ±1% at 25°C with B3435, but B tolerance and full resistance/temperature table are unspecified. Battery connector mapping, charging and battery mechanical release remain blocked.
