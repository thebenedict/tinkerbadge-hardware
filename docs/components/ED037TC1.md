# ED037TC1

Electrical candidate defined by the exact 19-page Waveshare 3.7-inch Rev 1.0 specification. The ED037TC1 / SSD1677 association does not establish the next shipped panel revision. The panel includes its controller. Use switched 3.3 V on pins 15/16; pin 18 is a separate 1.8 V regulator node. Pins 1/4 remain NC, and pin 5 is bypassed VSH2 rather than the generic HAT VGH label. RESE and SCL are inputs despite errors in the panel table’s I/O column. Pixel review covered the actual flex diagram, pin table, HAT circuit, and controller circuit.

See [display circuit decisions](../design/display.md) for voltage margins, support values, boot/off-state behavior, and release gates.
