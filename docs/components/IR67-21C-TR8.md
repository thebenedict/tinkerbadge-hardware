## Limits and working baseline

940nm emitter,120° full viewing angle. At20mA, VF1.2V typical/1.5V maximum. Continuous IF65mA and power130mW at25°C, with thermal derating. Pulsed1A absolute limit requires≤100µs and≤1% duty; that rating must not be used for a33–50% duty38kHz carrier.

D501 anode fed from3.3V through100Ω1%0805≥0.25W resistor; cathode to AO3400A drain. Nominal about21mA with1.2V VF. Even with zero LED drop and3.6V rail the electrical bound is36.4mA, below25°C continuous limit. Worst resistor short-LED dissipation3.6²/99≈131mW, hence≥0.25W. Board use is initially0–45°C; thermal and optical range still require assembled testing. Use38kHz carrier, one-third carrier duty and bounded packet duty. All gate defaults low; storage emits nothing. This is a working option for optical experiments, not a certified high-power IR product.
