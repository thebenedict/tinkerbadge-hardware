## Electrical and support requirements

- Pages2–4: supply3.3–5.3V; input absolute −0.3V to VDD+0.7V; VIH minimum0.55×VDD; VIL maximum0.7V. These are manufacturer limits. The project prototype exception below does not extend this range; do not rely on promotional overvoltage-survival claims.
- 5mA nominal per color; eight full-white pixels draw approximately120mA before tolerance. Dark standby≤1µA/pixel under stated5V test. PWM about2kHz.
- Eight fitted LEDs D401–D408, VDD on the same nominal +3V3 supply as the MCU, with100nF10V X7R at each and22µF10V X7R local bulk. First DI has330Ω series damping and100kΩ ground pulldown; intermediate DO→DI direct; final DO explicit NC. Decoupling follows p5 typical connection; bulk/damping/pulldown are project choices to scope-test.
- Within the rated supply range, the shared rail makes the LED VIH requirement (0.55 times VDD) track the MCU guaranteed high level (at least 0.8 times VDD). Retain the LED VIL maximum of 0.7 V. No level shifter is fitted.
- **User-approved prototype exception:** nominal 3.3 V with regulator tolerance, distribution loss and transient droop does not guarantee the documented 3.3 V LED minimum. Operation and electrical thresholds below that minimum are not guaranteed. This is an explicit prototype experiment, not a revised manufacturer rating. Measure startup, low battery, Wi-Fi activity and full-white LED load transitions at the actual LED supply pins; verify shared peak-current capacity and recovery. Revisit the power architecture if the tests show unreliable behavior.

## Firmware, reset and sleep

GRB, MSB first. T0H220–380ns, T1H580–1000ns; T0L/T1L580–1000ns; reset low>280µs (pp3–4). A working800kbit/s starting point is0-bit high0.30µs/low0.95µs and1-bit high0.65µs/low0.60µs; scope the actual pin. Initialize all black after stable rails and send black before storage. No LED rail gate is fitted. Hold data low during reset/sleep to avoid floating input. LEDs and MCU now share the same supply ramp. Keep the first DIN pulldown and hold the MCU output low until the shared rail has settled. Do not independently power the LED chain. Test reset/data behavior, shared-rail transients, recovery and black leakage on the assembled board.
