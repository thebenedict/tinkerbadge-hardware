# AO3400A component facts

## Source and physical package

[AO3400A.pdf](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/datasheets/AO3400A.pdf), Rev3.1,July2023. [Source](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/datasheets/AO3400A.pdf). URL, retrieval route and SHA256 are in `AO3400A.source.json` under datasheets.

SOT-23 N-channel MOSFET. No EP. Numeric1=G,2=S,3=D is derived from the indexed G terminal and conventional SOT23 numbering, rather than a printed AOS numbered table. Independent reviewer acceptance is required.

Inspected actual datasheet p1 G/S/D-labelled top/bottom views and separate [AOS SOT23 package drawing](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/package/SOT23.pdf), PO-00001 VersionN, p1. Drawing source: https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/package/SOT23.pdf . Hash in AOS-SOT23.source.json. Neither source prints numbers1/2/3. The index dot is adjacent to G; conventional SOT23 indexing gives1G,2S,3D. Datasheet p5 is test waveforms, not a package drawing; the earlier citation was incorrect. Package body nominal2.90×1.60mm, overall lead span2.80mm, height≤1.25mm, adjacent pitch0.95mm. Recommended three land pads0.80mm square, row-centers2.40mm.

## Limits and project connection

VDS30V and VGS±12V absolute maximum. Maximum RDS(on)48mΩ atVGS2.5V; do not infer suitable gate drive from VGS(th). IR driver Q501 sourceGND, drainIR_LED_K, gateIR_GATE. MCU IR_TX reaches gate through100Ω;100kΩ gate-source holds it off at reset/sleep. Gate drive is3.3V and the IR current is about20mA, far below transistor thermal capability. Body diode points source→drain and does not conduct normally in this low-side connection.

A second use for input-presence detection may use the same facts; its gate divider and worst input/clamp voltage remain the power sheet's contract. Never expose gate directly to unchecked external voltage.
