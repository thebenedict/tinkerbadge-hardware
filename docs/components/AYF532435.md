# AYF532435

Preferred physical connector candidate. Official Y5B drawings establish dual contacts, 0.5 mm pitch, 0.30 ± 0.03 mm FPC thickness, 50 V / 0.5 A ratings, and the dimensional pattern. They do not label numbered terminals. Numbers below are project panel-flex positions. J301 remains an electrical flex model with no approved footprint until the contact face, board view, fold, and pad-1 continuity are checked. The Hirose bottom-contact alternative is not approved.

See [display circuit decisions](../design/display.md) for voltage margins, support values, boot/off-state behavior, and release gates.
